Puzzlewump – Privacy Policy

App: Puzzlewump: Puzzle Adventure (package com.puzzlewump.app) · Effective date: 16 November 2026

Puzzlewump is a puzzle game that works completely offline. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

Puzzlewump does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not request those permissions.

Internet access

The release version of the app is built without the Internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network.

Data kept on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, badges, the hero you picked and your settings such as sound and music) is saved only on your own device. It never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress inside the app, or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your phone settings.

Permissions

The app may use the device vibration feature to give small haptic feedback during play. It does not need any other permission.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, analytics tools or other third-party services that receive your data.

Children

The game is suitable for children and for grown-ups. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
