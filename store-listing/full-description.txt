Puzzlewump is a colourful puzzle adventure that trains memory, maths and logic, one fun level at a time.

Join the friendly pink brain and pick your hero (Max, Mia, Coco or Robo) to explore 5 magical worlds with 150 levels:

• Memory Kingdom: flip magic cards, repeat royal tunes and remember where the treasures hide.
• Number Planet: blast through sums, find missing numbers and crack star patterns.
• Mystery Jungle: plan your way through twisty jungle paths and push stones to open the temple.
• Logic Galaxy: spot the odd alien, complete star patterns and rotate cosmic shapes.
• Treasure Island: spot the differences, count coins and follow the map to the golden treasure.

Every world ends with a friendly boss puzzle to beat!

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 150 levels that get gently harder as you play
• Earn up to 3 stars per level, collect coins and open treasure chests
• A new Daily Challenge every day, a streak to keep and daily login rewards
• 22 badges to earn and heroes to unlock
• Hints when you get stuck
• Bright 3D-style art, cheerful music and sound effects

MADE FOR PEACE OF MIND
• Works 100% offline, no internet needed
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• No accounts, no tracking and no personal data collected
• Progress is saved only on your device
• Music, sound and vibration can be switched on or off in Settings

Great for curious kids, families and anyone who loves a good brain puzzle.
